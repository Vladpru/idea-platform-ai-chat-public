# How to Submit a Problem

## Overview

A problem describes a real challenge or pain point that the team wants to address. Problems are the foundation of the platform — ideas must be linked to at least one existing problem. If you have a solution in mind but no matching problem exists yet, create the problem first.

To open the problem submission form, click **Add New Problem** in the top navigation bar or use the quick action on the home page.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1 — Write a clear title

The title should describe the challenge in one sentence (up to 200 characters). Good titles are specific and describe the pain point, not the solution.

Example: "Teams miss important updates because there is no weekly digest"

Use **Generate title from description** to let AI suggest a title once you have written a description.

### Step 2 — Write a description

Explain the problem in detail — what is happening, who is affected, and why it matters. Aim for 2–3 paragraphs (up to 5000 characters).

Use **Write description with AI prompt** to expand a short prompt into a full description.

### Step 3 — Write a value proposition

Explain what solving this problem would unlock for the team or organisation. This helps others understand the impact and prioritise accordingly.

Use **Write value proposition with AI prompt** to draft this section with AI assistance.

### Step 4 — Save as draft or publish

- **Save as draft** — the problem is private and only visible to you. You can continue editing from your profile under **Drafts**.
- **Publish** — the problem becomes visible to all authenticated users and can receive ideas linked to it.

You can edit your own problem within 24 hours of publishing. After that, contact an admin to request an edit.

## After Publishing

Once published, other users can:

- Link their ideas to your problem
- Comment and vote on it
- Join the problem to signal they are working on it

You can update the problem's stage at any time from the problem detail page.

## Stages

Problems move through the same lifecycle stages as ideas:

- **Ideation** — problem identified, gathering interest and ideas
- **Prototyping** — solutions are being actively explored
- **Completed** — the problem has been resolved
- **Paused** — temporarily on hold
- **Archived** — no longer active
