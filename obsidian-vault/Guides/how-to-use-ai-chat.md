# How to Use the AI Assistant

## Overview

The AI assistant is a built-in chat interface that helps you explore, understand, and contribute to the Idea Platform. You can ask it questions about existing ideas and problems, request summaries, compare entries, or ask it to draft a new idea or problem for you.

The assistant is available as a floating button on every page, or as a full-page experience via the AI Chat section in the navigation.

## What You Can Ask

### Search and browse

Ask the assistant to find ideas or problems by topic, keyword, or theme.

Examples:
- "Show me all ideas about sustainability"
- "Find problems related to onboarding"
- "List ideas in the Prototyping stage"

### Explain

Ask for a detailed explanation of a specific idea or problem.

Examples:
- "Explain the Smart Parking idea"
- "What is the Slow Onboarding problem about?"

### Compare

Ask the assistant to compare two or more ideas or problems side by side.

Examples:
- "Compare the two mobility ideas"
- "What are the differences between these two onboarding proposals?"

### Summarise

Ask for a summary of recent activity or a group of entries.

Examples:
- "What happened this week?"
- "Summarise all ideas in the energy category"

### Navigate the platform

Ask for help finding features or understanding how the platform works.

Examples:
- "How do I submit a problem?"
- "Where can I find my drafts?"
- "How do I join an idea?"

### Draft a new idea

Ask the assistant to create an idea draft for you. It will extract a title, description, and suggested tags from your message and open a draft card for you to confirm before anything is saved.

Examples:
- "Create an idea about smart parking sensors"
- "I want to propose a weekly digest for team updates"
- "Draft an idea for an AI-powered onboarding assistant"

The draft is not saved until you click **Create Draft** on the confirmation card. You can dismiss it if you change your mind.

### Draft a new problem

Ask the assistant to create a problem draft. It will extract a title and description and open a confirmation card.

Examples:
- "Submit a problem about slow onboarding for new users"
- "I want to raise a problem about missing notifications"

The draft is saved as private until you publish it from the problem detail page.

## Tips

- The assistant uses the platform's knowledge base to answer questions. If something is not indexed yet, it will say so.
- For follow-up questions, you can use natural language like "tell me more about the second one" — the assistant tracks conversation context.
- If the assistant misunderstands your intent, rephrase and try again.
- Use the full-page AI Chat for longer research sessions; use the floating widget for quick lookups without leaving your current page.
