# How to Submit an Idea

## Overview

An idea is a proposed solution to a problem. Before submitting, check if a similar idea already exists using the search feature — the platform also surfaces similar ideas automatically once you start writing.

To open the idea creation form, click **Add New Idea** in the top navigation bar or use the quick action on the home page.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1 — Select a stage

Choose the current stage of your idea from the **Stage** dropdown. New ideas default to **Ideation**. You can change the stage later as the idea progresses.

Available stages: Ideation, Prototyping, Completed, Paused, Archived.

### Step 2 — Link to a problem

Select the problem your idea addresses from the **Problem this idea solves** dropdown. This field is required. If no matching problem exists yet, create one first via **Add New Problem**.

### Step 3 — Write a clear title

The title should describe your solution in one sentence (up to 200 characters). Good titles are specific and action-oriented.

Example: "Automated weekly summary emails for team subscriptions"

Use the **Generate title from description** link to let AI suggest a title once you have written a description.

### Step 4 — Write your description

Explain what your idea does, how it works, and why it is valuable. The rich text editor supports bold, italic, and lists. Aim for 2–3 paragraphs (up to 5000 characters).

Use **Write description with AI prompt** to expand a short prompt into a full description, or click **Generate Draft** in the top-right to have AI produce a complete draft from scratch.

### Step 5 — Add a main image

Upload a cover image that represents your idea. If you do not have one ready, click **Generate image from title & description** to have AI create one based on your content.

### Step 6 — Add tags

Tags help others discover your idea. Select from existing tags or type a new name to create one. You can add up to 5 tags.

### Step 7 — Save as draft or publish

- **Save as draft** — the idea is private and only visible to you and admins. You can continue editing at any time from your profile under **Drafts**.
- **Publish** — the idea becomes visible to all authenticated users and can receive comments and votes.

Published ideas are visible to all authenticated users. Members can edit their own ideas within 24 hours of publishing.

## Lifecycle Stages

After publishing, your idea moves through stages:

- **Ideation** — initial proposal, gathering feedback
- **Prototyping** — active development or experimentation
- **Completed** — shipped or implemented
- **Paused** — temporarily on hold
- **Archived** — no longer active

You can update the stage at any time from the idea detail page.

## Collaborating on an idea

Other users can join your idea by clicking the **Join** button on the idea detail page. Joined members appear listed under the idea and the idea shows up in their **Joined** tab on their profile. This is useful for signalling active contributors and building visibility around shared efforts.
