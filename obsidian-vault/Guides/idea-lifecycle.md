# Idea and Problem Lifecycle

## Overview

Both ideas and problems move through lifecycle stages that reflect their current state of progress. Stages help the team understand what is being actively worked on, what is completed, and what has been paused or archived.

Authors can update the stage at any time from the detail page of their idea or problem.

## Stages

### Ideation

The starting stage for all new ideas and problems. The entry has been submitted and is open for feedback, comments, and votes. The team is still exploring whether and how to act on it.

Use this stage when:
- You have just submitted the idea or problem
- You are still gathering input from others
- No active development or prototyping has started yet

### Prototyping

The idea or problem is being actively worked on. A solution is being designed, built, or tested. This stage signals to others that progress is happening.

Use this stage when:
- A team or individual has picked up the idea and is building something
- Experiments or pilots are underway
- The problem is being actively investigated

### Completed

The idea has been implemented or the problem has been resolved. This stage marks a successful outcome.

Use this stage when:
- The proposed solution has been shipped or deployed
- The problem no longer exists or has been fully addressed
- The work is done and no further action is needed

### Paused

Work on the idea or problem has stopped temporarily. It may resume in the future.

Use this stage when:
- The team has deprioritised it due to capacity or strategy changes
- External blockers are preventing progress
- You want to keep it visible but signal it is not active right now

### Archived

The idea or problem is no longer active and will not be pursued. Archived entries remain visible and searchable but are read-only — they cannot receive new comments or votes.

Use this stage when:
- The idea is no longer relevant or has been superseded
- The problem has been resolved by other means
- The entry was submitted in error or is a duplicate

## Updating the Stage

Open the idea or problem detail page and use the **Stage** control to change the current stage. The change is saved immediately and visible to all users.

## Visibility by Stage

All published ideas and problems are visible to authenticated users regardless of their stage. Draft content is only visible to the author.
