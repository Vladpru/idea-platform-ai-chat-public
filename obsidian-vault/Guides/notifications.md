# Notifications

## Overview

The Idea Platform sends notifications to keep you informed about activity that is relevant to you. Notifications can be delivered in-app (visible when you open the platform) and by email. You control which types you receive and through which channel.

## Notification Types

### New similar ideas

Triggered when a new idea is published that is similar to one you have submitted. Useful for staying aware of overlapping proposals.

### New similar problems

Triggered when a new problem is posted that is similar to one you have submitted.

### Status change

Triggered when the stage of an idea or problem you follow changes — for example, when it moves from Ideation to Prototyping or is marked as Completed.

### New supporters

Triggered when someone follows you or joins an idea or problem you are involved in.

### New comments

Triggered when someone comments on an idea or problem you have submitted or joined.

### Assigned ideas and problems

Triggered when an idea or problem is assigned to you.

## Managing Notifications

Open the user menu (your avatar in the top-right corner) → **Settings → Notifications**.

Each notification type has two independent toggles:
- **In-App** — shows a notification badge and entry in the notifications panel
- **Email** — sends an email to your registered address

You can enable or disable each toggle individually.

### Disable all notifications

Click **Disable All** at the top of the Notifications settings page to turn off all notification types at once.

### Enable all notifications

Click **Enable All** at the top of the Notifications settings page to turn on all notification types at once.

## Email Notifications

Email notifications are sent to the address associated with your account. If you are not receiving emails, check your spam folder or verify your email address is correct in **Settings → Account**.

To stop all email notifications without affecting in-app notifications, disable the Email toggle for each type individually or use **Disable All** and then re-enable the In-App toggles you want to keep.
