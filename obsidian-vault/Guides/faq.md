# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
If you are logged out, go to the login page (`/login`) and click "FORGOT PASSWORD?" below the password field. Enter your registered email address and you will receive a password reset link. If you are already logged in, open the user menu (your avatar in the top-right corner), go to **Settings → Account**, and click **Change Password**.

**Can I change my email address?**
Email changes are not currently supported. Contact an admin if you need to migrate your account.

**How do I delete my account?**
Account deletion requests must be submitted to an admin. All your ideas and problems will be anonymised, not deleted.

**How do I edit my profile (name, photo, bio)?**
Open the user menu (top-right avatar) → **Settings → Account**, then click **Edit Profile**.

**How do I switch between light and dark mode?**
Open the user menu → **Settings → Appearance**. Choose Light, Dark, or System (follows your OS setting). The preference is synced to your account.

## Ideas

**Can I edit a published idea?**
Yes, within 24 hours of publishing. After that, contact an admin to request an edit.

**What happens when I archive an idea?**
Archived ideas remain visible but are marked as inactive. They cannot receive new comments or votes.

**Can two people collaborate on an idea?**
Yes. Any user can join an idea by clicking the **Join** button on the idea detail page. Joined members appear under the idea and the idea shows up in their **Joined** tab on their profile. Co-authors who have joined can contribute to the idea.

**What AI tools are available when creating an idea?**
The idea creation form includes several AI-powered shortcuts: **Generate Draft** (generates a full draft from scratch), **Generate title from description** (suggests a title based on what you have written), **Write description with AI prompt** (expands a short prompt into a full description), and **Generate image from title & description** (creates a cover image automatically).

**How many tags can I add to an idea?**
You can add up to 5 tags. You can select from existing tags already used on the platform or type a new tag name to create one.

## Problems

**What is the difference between a problem and an idea?**
A problem describes a challenge or pain point. An idea describes a proposed solution. When creating an idea you must link it to one or more existing problems.

**Who can submit a problem?**
Any authenticated user can submit a problem.

**Can I use AI when writing a problem?**
Yes. The problem submission form has **Generate title from description**, **Write description with AI prompt**, and **Write value proposition with AI prompt** buttons to help you draft content faster.

## Notifications

**How do I stop receiving email notifications?**
Open the user menu (top-right avatar) → **Settings → Notifications**. Each notification type has separate toggles for In-App and Email. You can turn off individual types or click **Disable All** to mute everything at once.

**What notification types can I configure?**
You can independently control the following types:
- **New similar ideas** — when a new idea similar to yours is published
- **New similar problems** — when a new problem similar to yours is posted
- **Status change** — when the status of an idea or problem you follow changes
- **New supporters** — when someone follows you or joins your idea/problem
- **New comments** — when someone comments on your content
- **Assigned ideas and problems** — when an admin assigns a project to you

**How do I enable all notifications at once?**
Go to **Settings → Notifications** and click **Enable All** at the top of the page.
