# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter your email address and you will receive a reset link within 5 minutes.

**Can I change my email address?**
Email changes are not currently supported. Contact an admin if you need to migrate your account.

**How do I delete my account?**
Account deletion requests must be submitted to an admin. All your ideas and problems will be anonymised, not deleted.

## Ideas

**Can I edit a published idea?**
Yes, within 24 hours of publishing. After that, contact an admin to request an edit.

**What happens when I archive an idea?**
Archived ideas remain visible but are marked as inactive. They cannot receive new comments or votes.

## Problems

**What is the difference between a problem and an idea?**
A problem describes a challenge or pain point. An idea describes a proposed solution. Ideas are linked to one or more problems.

**Who can submit a problem?**
Any authenticated user with a Member or Admin role can submit a problem.

## Notifications

**How do I stop receiving email notifications?**
Go to your profile settings and update your notification preferences. You can disable individual notification types or all emails at once.

**What events trigger notifications?**
You receive notifications when someone comments on your content, likes your idea or problem, or when someone you follow publishes new content.
