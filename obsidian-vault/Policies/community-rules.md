# Community Rules

## Core Principles

The Idea Platform is a space for constructive innovation. All participants are expected to engage respectfully and in good faith. The goal is to surface real problems and practical solutions — not to promote individuals or generate noise.

## Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed on the platform:

- Harassment or personal attacks against other users
- Spam or duplicate submissions
- Content that violates intellectual property rights
- False or misleading information presented as fact
- Content that promotes discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or nationality
- Self-promotional content that is not genuinely solving a problem

## Idea Submission Guidelines

Ideas must be original and not previously submitted by another user.

Each idea must be linked to at least one existing problem. If no matching problem exists, create one before submitting the idea.

Each idea should clearly describe the problem it solves, the proposed solution, and expected impact.

Ideas in draft status are private. Only published ideas appear in the public catalog.

## Commenting Guidelines

Comments should be constructive and relevant to the idea or problem being discussed.

Feedback should focus on the content, not the author. Personal criticism is not permitted.

## Enforcement

Violations of community rules may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban depending on severity.

Users can report violations using the flag button on any idea, problem, or comment.

Admin review of flagged content happens within 48 hours.

Admins may remove content, warn users, suspend accounts, or issue permanent bans at their discretion.
