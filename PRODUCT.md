# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Corporate innovation teams at mid-to-large companies — product managers, department leads, and employees capturing internal ideas and problems as part of structured innovation workflows. Users are desk-based, working collaboratively across departments. They open this tool as part of their regular work routine, not as a side hobby. The AI Chat feature specifically serves these users when they want to query internal knowledge bases about ideas and problems without manually searching.

## Product Purpose

Idea Platform is an internal innovation hub where teams capture **problems**, create **ideas** to solve them, and track progress through lifecycle stages (ideation → prototyping → completed). It centralises institutional knowledge, enables cross-team collaboration, and surfaces AI-assisted insights via an integrated chat interface grounded in the company's own idea/problem data.

Success looks like: teams consistently logging both problems and ideas, ideas visibly progressing through stages, and the AI chat reducing the friction of finding relevant prior work.

## Brand Personality

Confident · Structured · Trustworthy

The product is a professional tool for serious work. It should feel like a trusted internal system — authoritative without being bureaucratic, modern without being flashy. Voice is calm, clear, and precise. It does not try to delight or entertain; it helps people do their jobs well.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / Jira clone**: Blue-heavy enterprise defaults, dense ticket lists, no personality. The platform should feel intentional and designed, not assembled from a component kit with default colours.
- **Flashy AI product**: Gradient text, purple-pink hero sections, over-animated loading states. The AI is a feature, not the product's identity — it should feel integrated and calm, not promotional.
- **Consumer social app**: Rounded everything, pastel cards, engagement-bait UI. Too casual for a professional innovation platform.
- **Heavy analytics dashboard**: Data tables and charts dominating every screen. This is a collaboration and ideation tool first; data is a supporting layer, not the lead.

## Design Principles

1. **Tool confidence over decoration** — every visual element serves a task. If it doesn't help the user act, it doesn't exist.
2. **Calm authority** — the interface communicates competence through restraint: consistent spacing, deliberate typography, a palette that stays out of the way until it needs to signal something.
3. **Structure without rigidity** — information is organised and predictable, but the tool adapts to the task rather than forcing the user into a fixed flow.
4. **AI as a colleague, not a feature** — the AI chat and summaries should feel like a knowledgeable peer embedded in the workflow, not a chatbot bolted on.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — surface what the user needs now; keep complexity available but not in the way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as the baseline. All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable with visible focus states. Colour alone must never convey meaning — pair with labels or icons. Reduced-motion preference must be respected for all animations. Sufficient contrast ratios throughout, including muted and placeholder text.
